Done together with the user: an hour of real listening is the only real test of an endless piece.

**Scope**
- Listen for 30–60 minutes with the model-call log at hand.
- Judge coherence: do themes return, does the music drift and evolve without wandering or looping, are transitions smooth, does it ever stall?
- Tune together: the composer prompt, effort, chunk lengths, humanization constants and checker thresholds. Small adjustments land in this ticket's commit. Anything bigger becomes its own ticket, filed before this one closes.
- Measure the real-time factor and how often the holding pattern fires.

**Docs:** design.md → Composition and Playback (tuned values and why); design.md → Decision log.

**Done when:** the user is happy with an hour of unattended listening, and the changes and measurements are recorded.
