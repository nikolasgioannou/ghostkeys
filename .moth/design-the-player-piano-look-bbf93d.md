Done together with the user: the look is theirs to shape. The direction from planning is a retro player-piano roll: dark, candlelit, perforated paper scrolling as notes are punched; sleek with minimal chrome.

**Scope**
- Explore and agree on the palette, typography, layout of the three elements (roll, play/pause, chat input), and motion (scroll speed, how perforations appear, the reply fade).
- If fonts are added, research self-hosting (e.g. Fontsource) and their licences first. No third-party font CDN without asking.
- Record the result in a new design.md → Look section, and as Tailwind `@theme` tokens in `app.css`, replacing the minimal dark base.

**Docs:** design.md → Look (new section in Part 1).

**Done when:** the user signs off on the look, and the tokens and design.md → Look describe it.
